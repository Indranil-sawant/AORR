1. Executive Summary

Since the initial search setup, your website has been transformed from a single static page into a complete multi-service enterprise portal. It cleanly showcases AORR's 3 primary business verticals: Global Trade & Distribution, Tours & Travel Services, and Medical Tourism & Global Healthcare.

2. Core Work Modules Completed

🔷 Module A: Dedicated Business Pages (3-Pillar System)
What We Built: Designed brand new pages for tours-travel.html and medical-tourism.html, alongside an updated Homepage (index.html), Services overview, and About Us section.
Business Benefit: Travel and medical tourism clients get a dedicated corporate experience without trade clutter.
🔷 Module B: Visual Redesign & Eco-Green Theme
What We Built: Overhauled all 9 website pages into a cohesive Forest Green & Mint theme with 40+ high-definition graphics and optimized layouts.
Business Benefit: Projects an elite, eco-conscious global trade image that builds instant trust with buyers.
🔷 Module C: Interactive Catalog & Header Search
What We Built: Added dynamic category/subcategory filtering on products.html, pop-up enquiry modals, and a live auto-suggest search bar in the navbar.
Business Benefit: Buyers find exact products and submit detailed quotes in seconds.
🔷 Module D: Automated Lead Capture & PDF Questionnaire
What We Built: Linked all website forms directly to Google Sheets (Code.gs) with automated email alerts and added an interactive client questionnaire with instant PDF download.
Business Benefit: Every inquiry is saved instantly to your spreadsheet with anti-spam security.
🔷 Module E: Mobile Speed & Performance Optimization
What We Built: Converted all media to next-gen WebP format, implemented server caching (.htaccess), and added a smooth orbiting preloader screen.
Business Benefit: Ultra-fast page loading keeps visitors on your site longer and improves Google rankings.
