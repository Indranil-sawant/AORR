🗓️ Day-by-Day Sacred Itinerary

01
🌅 MORNING

Arrival in Solapur / Raichur & scenic drive towards the holy Krishna River banks.

🛕 AFTERNOON

Peaceful boat transfer across Krishna River to Kuravpur Island; seek divine blessings at Sri Pada Shri Vallabha Birthplace & Holy Tapobhoomi.

🪔 EVENING

Audumbar Tree circumambulation (Pradakshina), peaceful river sangam meditation and evening prayers; overnight stay in Kuravpur / Raichur.

🚐 Kuravpur → Mantralaya • Approx. 2.5 hours
02
🌅 MORNING

Scenic drive to holy Mantralaya on the sacred banks of Tungabhadra River.

🛕 AFTERNOON

Sacred Moola Brindavanam Darshan of revered saint Shri Guru Raghavendra Swamy.

🪔 EVENING

Panchamukhi Hanuman Temple visit and serene Tungabhadra River evening Aarti; overnight stay in Mantralaya.

🚐 Mantralaya → Gangapur • Approx. 4 hours
03
🌅 MORNING

Proceed to sacred Kshetra Gangapur, the sanctified seat of Shri Narasimha Saraswati.

🛕 AFTERNOON

Nirgun Math Darshan of Lord Dattatreya Holy Nirguna Paduka and temple complex prayers.

🌊 EVENING

Sacred Sangam Snan at Bhima - Amarja river confluence & Audumbar Puja; overnight stay in Gangapur.

🚐 Gangapur → Akkalkot • Approx. 1.5 hours
04
🌅 MORNING

Drive to the divine pilgrimage town of Akkalkot.

🛕 AFTERNOON

Shri Swami Samarth Maharaj Vatavruksha Mandir Darshan and Samadhi shrine prayers.

🪔 EVENING

Partake in Annachhatra Mahaprasad and witness the evening Palkhi Seva procession; overnight stay in Akkalkot.

🚐 Akkalkot → Solapur • Approx. 1 hour
05
🌅 MORNING

Proceed to Solapur (known as the Pandhari of the South).

🛕 AFTERNOON

Ancient Shri Siddheshwar Temple Darshan and serene lake visit.

🛍️ EVENING

Explore world-famous Solapur handloom textile & chaddar market; tour concludes with drop at Solapur Railway Station / Bus Stand.

🗓️ Day-by-Day Sacred Itinerary

01
🌅 MORNING

Pithapuram Railway Station pickup and comfortable hotel check-in.

🛕 AFTERNOON

Darshan at Sri Pada Sri Vallabha Mahasansthanam (1st Incarnation of Lord Dattatreya) & Rudrabhisheka Puja.

🪔 EVENING

Visit ancient Kukuteswara (Shiva) & Kunti Madhava (Vishnu) Temples; participate in evening Palkhi Seva procession and overnight stay.

🚐 Pithapuram → Annavaram → Uppada → Kakinada • Approx. 1.5–2 hours
02
🌅 MORNING

Sri Veera Venkata Satyanarayana Swamy Temple Darshan atop Ratnagiri Hill, Annavaram.

🛕 AFTERNOON

Perform holy Satyanarayana Vratham Pooja; explore the world-renowned Uppada Jamdani handloom weavers.

🏛️ EVENING

Visit Samalkota Kumararama Bheemeshwara Swamy (Pancharama) Temple; transfer to Kakinada for overnight stay.

🚐 Kakinada → Draksharamam → Kotipalli • Approx. 1 hour
03
🌅 MORNING

Draksharamam Pancharama Bhimeswara Swamy Temple Darshan (revered as Daksha Vatika).

🛕 AFTERNOON

Kotipalli Someswara Swamy Temple on the sacred Godavari river & Sarpavaram Bhavanarayana Swamy Temple.

🌊 EVENING

Visit Coringa Mangrove Nature Reserve (optional boating) and enjoy sunset relaxation at Kakinada Beach; overnight stay in Kakinada.

🚐 Kakinada → Ainavilli → Antarvedi → Rajahmundry • Approx. 2.5 hours
04
🌅 MORNING

Ainavilli Sri Siddhi Vinayaka Swamy Temple Darshan for divine blessings.

🛕 AFTERNOON

Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Temple Darshan at sacred Antarvedi.

🌊 EVENING

Prayers & holy snan at the sacred Godavari River - Bay of Bengal confluence (Sagara Sangam); proceed to Rajahmundry for overnight stay.

🚐 Antarvedi → Rajamahendravaram (Rajahmundry) • Approx. 2 hours
05
🌅 MORNING

Visit sacred Godavari River Ghats (Pushkar Ghat) and ancient Kotilingeshwara Temple Darshan.

🛍️ AFTERNOON

Leisure time for shopping authentic Rajahmundry sweets (Pootharekulu) and local cultural souvenirs.

✈️ EVENING

Transfer drop to Rajahmundry Airport / Railway Station with divine memories and spiritual blessings.

🗓️ Day-by-Day Sacred & Historic Itinerary

01
🌅 MORNING

Arrival in Bijapur; visit the magnificent Gol Gumbaz with its world-famous acoustic Whispering Gallery.

🏛️ AFTERNOON

Explore the historic Ibrahim Rouza and ancient Adil Shahi architectural landmarks.

🛕 EVENING

Darshan at Shiv Giri with its awe-inspiring 85-foot giant Lord Shiva statue; overnight stay in Bijapur.

🚐 Bijapur → Almatti Dam → Kudalsangama → Hospet/Hampi • Approx. 3 hours
02
🌅 MORNING

Visit massive Almatti Dam on Krishna River, featuring Mughal-style landscaped gardens and musical fountains.

🛕 AFTERNOON

Visit Kudalsangama, the sacred confluence of Krishna & Malaprabha rivers, and the holy Aikya Mantapa of Saint Basaveshwara.

🏨 EVENING

Drive to Hospet / Hampi and complete hotel check-in; overnight stay in Hampi / Hospet.

🚐 Hospet → Hampi Heritage Circuit • UNESCO World Heritage Site
03
🌅 MORNING

Explore the grand Virupaksha Temple on Hemakuta Hill and the holy Tungabhadra riverfront.

🏛️ AFTERNOON

Visit the world-famous Stone Chariot, musical pillars of Vijaya Vittala Temple complex, and King's Balance.

🌅 EVENING

Tour Lotus Mahal, Elephant Stables, Queen's Bath, and enjoy a magnificent sunset over boulder landscapes; overnight in Hampi / Hospet.

🚐 Hampi → Tungabhadra Dam → Aihole → Badami • Approx. 2.5 hours
04
🌅 MORNING

Visit Tungabhadra Dam reservoir viewpoints, lush Japanese landscaped gardens, and deer park.

🏛️ AFTERNOON

Drive to Aihole, the cradle of Hindu rock architecture, showcasing 125+ 6th-century stone temples (Durga Temple & Lad Khan).

🏨 EVENING

Transfer to Badami for hotel check-in and relaxing evening; overnight stay in Badami.

🚐 Badami → Pattadakal & Badami Caves • Approx. 30 mins
05
🌅 MORNING

Visit Pattadakal (UNESCO World Heritage Site), featuring 7th & 8th-century Chalukyan Rekha-Nagara and Dravidian temples along the Malaprabha River.

🛕 AFTERNOON

Explore the 6th-century rock-cut cave temples of Badami and Bhutanatha Temple overlooking sacred Agastya Lake.

✈️ EVENING

Transfer drop to Hubli / Belgaum / Bijapur Railway Station or Airport with unforgettable golden heritage memories.
