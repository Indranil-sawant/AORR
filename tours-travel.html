🪔 GURU PARAMPARA TOUR 02

DIVINE DATTATREYA & GURU PILGRIMAGE — 5 DAYS / 4 NIGHTS

Solapur • Akkalkot • Gangapur • Kuravpur • Mantralaya • Solapur
⏱️ 5 Days / 4 Nights
📍 Curated Tour Route & Circuit: Solapur → Akkalkot → Gangapur → Kuravpur (Island Shrine) → Mantralaya → Solapur

🗓️ Day-by-Day Guru Kshetra Itinerary

DAY 1
Solapur → Akkalkot → Gangapur
  • Morning pickup at Solapur
  • Akkalkot (40 km): Shri Swami Samarth Maharaj Vatavruksha Mandir & Samadhi
  • Proceed to Gangapur (110 km)
  • Evening Darshan at Shri Kshetra Gangapur
  • Overnight stay in Gangapur
DAY 2
Gangapur → Kuravpur
  • Pre-dawn Nirgun Math Darshan & Audumbar Tree Puja
  • Sangam Snan at Bhima - Amarja River Confluence
  • Proceed to Kuravpur via local boat transfer across Krishna River
  • Darshan at Shri Pada Shri Vallabha Birthplace & Tapobhoomi
  • Overnight stay in Kuravpur / Raichur
DAY 3
Kuravpur / Raichur → Mantralaya
  • Morning prayers and check-out
  • Scenic drive to Mantralaya (150 km)
  • Arrival at Mantralaya — holy abode of Shri Raghavendra Swamy
  • Evening Moola Brindavanam Darshan & Temple illuminations
  • Overnight stay in Mantralaya
DAY 4
Mantralaya Local Darshan & Seva
  • Full day dedicated to Brindavanam Darshan, Pooja & Seva
  • Panchamukhi Hanuman Temple visit
  • Sacred Snan & Aarti at Tungabhadra River Ghats
  • Overnight stay in Mantralaya
DAY 5
Mantralaya → Solapur Sightseeing → Drop
  • Return drive towards Solapur
  • Solapur Sightseeing: Ancient Siddheshwar Temple & Lake
  • Bhuikot Fort & Hutatma Smarak memorial
  • Solapur Handloom & Textile Market shopping
  • Final drop at Solapur Station / Bus Stand
🚘 Available Vehicle Options:
• Option 1 (Sedan): Suitable for small families (1-4 Passengers) • Option 2 (SUV): Suitable for families & small groups (5-7 Passengers) • Option 3 (Tempo Traveller): Suitable for larger groups (9-17 Passengers)

🕉️ MAHARASHTRA & KARNATAKA YATRA 03

DIVINE DATTA & VITTHAL PILGRIMAGE — 5 DAYS / 4 NIGHTS

Solapur • Akkalkot • Gangapur • Pandharpur • Tuljapur • Solapur (दिव्य दत्त आणि विठ्ठल तीर्थयात्रा)
⏱️ 5 Days / 4 Nights
📍 Sacred Route: Solapur → Akkalkot → Gangapur → Pandharpur → Tuljapur → Solapur
DAY 1
Solapur → Akkalkot (40 km)
  • Arrival in Solapur and drive to Akkalkot
  • Shri Swami Samarth Maharaj Vatavruksha Mandir Darshan
  • Swami Samarth Samadhi Shrine & Annachhatra
  • Evening Aarti & Palkhi Darshan
  • Overnight stay in Akkalkot
DAY 2
Akkalkot → Gangapur (110 km)
  • Proceed to Shri Kshetra Gangapur
  • Nirgun Math & Shri Dattatreya Nirguna Paduka Darshan
  • Audumbar Tree Puja & Bhima - Amarja Sangam Snan
  • Overnight stay in Gangapur
DAY 3
Gangapur → Pandharpur (200+ km)
  • Drive to the holy Warkari center of Pandharpur
  • Shri Vitthal - Rukmini Temple Darshan
  • Chandrabhaga River Snan & Pundalik Temple
  • Namdev Payari & local saint shrines
  • Overnight stay in Pandharpur
DAY 4
Pandharpur → Tuljapur (110 km)
  • Morning drive to Tuljapur Shaktipeeth
  • Kulswamini Shri Tulja Bhavani Mata Darshan
  • Explore ancient temple complex & Ghatila Darshan
  • Evening Aarti & Special Seva
  • Overnight stay in Tuljapur
DAY 5
Tuljapur → Solapur (45 km)
  • Early morning Tulja Bhavani Darshan & Check-out
  • Proceed to Solapur for Shri Siddheshwar Temple & Lake
  • Shopping for Solapur famous Chaddars & Handlooms
  • Tour concludes with final drop at Solapur

🏛️ UNESCO HERITAGE TOUR 04

KARNATAKA HERITAGE & SPIRITUAL EXPEDITION — 5 DAYS / 4 NIGHTS

History • UNESCO Architecture • Nature • Temples
⏱️ 5 Days / 4 Nights
📍 Heritage Route: Solapur → Bijapur → Shiv Giri → Almatti → Badami → Banashankari → Pattadakal → Hampi → Kudalasangama
HIGHLIGHT 1
Hampi & Pattadakal UNESCO Monuments

Marvel at the grand Vijayanagara Empire stone chariot, Virupaksha temple, and 7th-century Pattadakal structural temples.

HIGHLIGHT 2
Badami Caves & Banashankari

6th-century Chalukyan rock-cut cave temples cut into sandstone cliffs overlooking Agastya Lake.

HIGHLIGHT 3
Bijapur Gol Gumbaz & Shiv Giri

Acoustic whispering gallery of Gol Gumbaz dome and giant 85-foot Lord Shiva statue at Shiv Giri.

HIGHLIGHT 4
Almatti Gardens & Kudalasangama

Beautiful Mughal-style Almatti Dam gardens and sacred river confluence of Krishna & Malaprabha.
