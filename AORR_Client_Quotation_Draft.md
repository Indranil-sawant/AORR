### 1. Executive Summary

Since the initial Google Analytics setup, your website has undergone a complete transformation. What started as a basic online company brochure has now been expanded into a **modern, multi-service international business portal**. 

The upgraded website highlights all three of AORR’s core business pillars under one clean brand:
1. **Global Trade & Export** (Marine supplies, industrial parts, garments, agricultural items)
2. **Tours & Travel Division** (Corporate trips, holiday packages, flights, hotel bookings)
3. **Medical Tourism & Global Healthcare** (Connecting international patients with top accredited Indian hospitals)

---

### 2. Core Work Modules Completed

#### 🔷 Module A: Complete Business Expansion & New Web Pages
* **What We Built:** Created standalone, dedicated pages for **Tours & Travel** (`tours-travel.html`) and **Medical Tourism** (`medical-tourism.html`), while enhancing the main homepage (`index.html`), Services page (`services.html`), and About Us page (`about-us.html`).
* **Why It Matters to Your Business:** Customers visiting for travel or healthcare now get a dedicated, high-end experience without getting confused by trade products.

#### 🔷 Module B: Brand Redesign & Fresh Visual Theme
* **What We Built:** Updated the entire look and feel of the site with a clean, professional eco-green palette (Forest Green & Mint Accent), clear typography, modern layout boxes, and 40+ high-definition optimized images.
* **Why It Matters to Your Business:** Gives your company an elite, trustworthy, and eco-conscious global corporate presence that leaves a strong first impression on international clients.

#### 🔷 Module C: Dynamic Product Catalog & Smart Instant Search
* **What We Built:** Built an interactive product showroom (`products.html`) where visitors can filter items by category (e.g., Marine Supplies, Valves, Garments), click to view detailed product information, and use a smart search bar built directly into the top navigation menu.
* **Why It Matters to Your Business:** Buyers can find exact product specifications within seconds and request quotes instantly without searching manually.

#### 🔷 Module D: Automated Lead Generation & Instant Client Questionnaire
* **What We Built:** Linked all contact forms across the website directly to Google Sheets via Google Apps Script (`Code.gs`). Additionally created an interactive client questionnaire (`client-questionnaire.html`) that allows users to fill in project requirements and export a downloadable PDF summary instantly.
* **Why It Matters to Your Business:** Every inquiry is automatically saved in your spreadsheet and emailed to your team immediately. Built-in spam filters prevent junk messages from cluttering your inbox.

#### 🔷 Module E: Website Speed & Mobile Optimization
* **What We Built:** Converted all website images into modern WebP format, added smooth loading animations (`loader.js`), and optimized web server settings (`.htaccess`) for lightning-fast loading speeds on mobile phones and tablets.
* **Why It Matters to Your Business:** Faster loading websites hold visitor attention longer and rank significantly higher on Google.

#### 🔷 Module F: Search Engine Setup (SEO) & Technical Documentation
* **What We Built:** Created updated search maps (`sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`), search engine tags (Open Graph / Schema), and detailed technical guides (`docs/` directory & `AGENTS.md`).
* **Why It Matters to Your Business:** Ensures search engines like Google index all your pages properly, and provides clear blueprints for future updates.
