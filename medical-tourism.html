Transparent & Ethical Patient Coordination

AORR operates strictly as an independent healthcare facilitator and travel logistics coordinator. We provide comprehensive travel arrangements, hospital scheduling, and on-ground patient support. We do not provide medical diagnosis, medical treatments, or clinical advice directly; all medical consultations, treatment options, and clinical evaluations are conducted exclusively by licensed physicians at accredited hospitals.
