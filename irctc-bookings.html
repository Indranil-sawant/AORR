FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Railway Booking FAQs

Clear answers to your most common questions about Indian Railways ticket reservations with AORR.

01. What are the Tatkal booking timings and how does AORR help?
Tatkal booking opens precisely one day prior to the train's originating departure date: 10:00 AM for AC Classes (1AC, 2AC, 3AC, CC, EC) and 11:00 AM for Non-AC / Sleeper Class. Because seats sell out within minutes, our authorized rail desk coordinates your passenger details in advance and submits booking requests promptly at opening second to maximize confirmation success.
02. How do I receive my official train ticket after booking?
Once booked by our official IRCTC agent portal, the genuine government electronic ticket (PDF format with PNR and QR code) is dispatched directly to your WhatsApp number and Email address. You can simply show the PDF ticket on your mobile alongside your government photo ID while boarding.
03. Can AORR arrange block train bookings for spiritual yatras or weddings?
Yes! As an authorized agent working alongside our Datta Chaya Tourism division, we specialize in coordinated block bookings for large family groups, spiritual sanghs, wedding guests, and corporate delegates across sleeper and AC coaches.
04. What government identification is required for railway booking?
During booking, we require each passenger's full name, exact age, gender, and berth preference. While traveling, at least one passenger must carry a valid original Government photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License, or PAN Card).
05. What if my ticket is Waitlisted (WL) or RAC?
Our rail desk continuously tracks your PNR confirmation probability right up to chart preparation (typically 4 hours before departure). If confirmation odds are low, our experts suggest alternative trains, neighboring stations, or Tatkal re-booking to ensure you do not miss your trip.
