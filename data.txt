Product Categories & Inventory
AORR focuses on six primary categories for import and export. The company connects global buyers and sellers to ensure high-quality products reach various regions.
1. Agro-Commodities AORR trades in a variety of raw and processed agricultural goods.
• Nuts: Raw Cashew Nuts, Cashew Kernels, Macadamia Nuts, and Almonds,,,.
• Spices & Flavors: Vanilla, Cocoa, and various Spices,.
• Seeds: Sesame Seeds.
• Sugar: Specifically ICUMSA 45 Sugar, a highly refined sugar grade,.
2. FMCG (Fast Moving Consumer Goods)
• Overview: These are everyday necessities sold quickly at relatively low prices. AORR acts as a platform to bridge the supply and demand gap for these constant-demand items.
• Inventory: Packaged consumer goods and daily essentials.
3. General Trading This category covers a diverse range of utility items, as depicted in the company's catalog images:
• Household Items: Ceiling fans, table fans, and sewing machines.
• Lifestyle & Fitness: Bicycles and exercise equipment.
4. Machinery and Vehicles AORR deals in heavy equipment, primarily focused on the agricultural sector:
• Inventory: Tractors, harvesters, and trailers.
5. Metal and Alloys
• Inventory: Scrap metal, coils, aluminum, and various metal alloys.
6. Woods
• Inventory: Timber logs and processed wood.
