Who We Are

Professional global trade solutions built on expertise and integrity

AORR Global Trading & Distribution

AORR is a global trading and consulting company based in Pune, India, specializing in international commerce across Marine & Ship materials, Industrial commercial goods, and Domestic supply. We help businesses navigate complex trade regulations, source quality products, and execute cross-border transactions efficiently.

What Problems We Solve:
We address the core challenges businesses face in international trade: navigating customs regulations, ensuring product quality, managing logistics, maintaining compliance, and establishing reliable supplier networks. Our structured approach removes uncertainty from global commerce.

Who We Serve:
We work with shipbuilders, marine engineers, industrial manufacturers, commercial key trading partners, and domestic distributors. Our clients range from established enterprises to growing MSMEs seeking international market access.

QUALITY COMMITMENT

Our Commitment to Quality & Support

Ensuring excellence at every stage of the trade process

Quality Assurance

Every shipment undergoes rigorous quality checks before dispatch. We work only with verified suppliers who meet international standards. Product samples, quality certificates, and third-party inspection reports are standard practice.

Documentation Excellence

Complete documentation is provided for every transaction: commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, quality certificates, and customs declarations. All records are maintained for audit and compliance purposes.

On-Time Delivery

We maintain a 99.9% on-time delivery record through proactive logistics planning, real-time shipment tracking, and contingency management. Delays are communicated immediately with alternative solutions.

Ongoing Support

Our relationship extends beyond delivery. We assist with documentation queries, provide market updates, help resolve post-delivery issues, and maintain readiness for your next requirement.
