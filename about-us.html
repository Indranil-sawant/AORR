AORR Global Trading

AORR is a global trading and consulting company dedicated to helping businesses navigate the complexities of international commerce. Based in Pune, we combine regional expertise with a global outlook to deliver practical, compliant, and scalable trade solutions.

We specialize in identifying opportunities within complex markets and transforming them into sustainable business outcomes through disciplined execution and strong governance.

Our approach is built on deep market knowledge, regulatory compliance, and operational excellence. We work across agro-commodities, FMCG, and industrial sectors, ensuring that each transaction meets international standards while delivering measurable value to our partners.
