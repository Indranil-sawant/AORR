FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers For Global Enterprises & Pilgrims

Quick guidance regarding our specialized divisions: Global Trade & Logistics, Datta Chaya Spiritual Yatras, AORR IRCTC Rail Desk, and Medical Tourism Concierge.

01. What verticals operate under AORR Global Enterprise? ▼
AORR Global Enterprise operates as a premier master corporate umbrella based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. It unites three specialized, distinct business divisions:

1. Global Trade & Logistics: Exporting marine raw materials, shipbuilding resins, industrial valves, agro commodities, and garments to 50+ countries.
2. Tours, Travel & Rail Services: Handcrafted spiritual yatra circuits by Datta Chaya Tourism (Andhra, Karnataka, Pandharpur, Guru Parampara), alongside an independent Authorised IRCTC Rail Desk by AORR.
3. Medical Tourism Concierge: Connecting international patients with top NABH/JCI accredited super-specialty hospitals, medical visa letters (VIL), and 24/7 dedicated care managers.
02. How do I book train tickets with the AORR IRCTC Rail Desk? ▼
AORR is an official Authorised IRCTC Railway Agent. You can book 1AC, 2AC, 3AC, Sleeper, and Tatkal train tickets with expert routing and seat availability guidance directly by contacting our rail desk via WhatsApp (+91 9730004911) or through our online inquiry form. We deliver fast official e-tickets with complete journey support.
03. What makes Datta Chhaya Tourism pilgrimage packages special? ▼
Our 4 signature pilgrimage and heritage tour packages — Divine Dattatreya & Guru Pilgrimage (5D/4N), Divine Andhra (5D/4N), Divine Datta & Vitthal Pilgrimage (2D/3N), and Karnataka Heritage Tour (4N/5D) — include comfortable private AC vehicles (Sedan / SUV), verified hotel accommodations, all-places temple darshan & sightseeing coordination, daily breakfast (as per package), and dedicated support throughout your journey.
04. What quality standards and customs compliance does AORR Global Trade adhere to? ▼
Our export operations strictly comply with 100% AEO (Authorized Economic Operator) customs standards and ISO quality protocols. All marine supplies, shipbuilding raw materials, marine timber, resins, and high-pressure industrial valves undergo rigorous pre-shipment lab quality testing and factory-to-port inspection prior to dispatch.
05. How does AORR assist international patients seeking medical treatment in India? ▼
Our ethical medical tourism concierge provides end-to-end patient assistance: free doctor case evaluation from accredited NABH/JCI super-specialists, transparent treatment cost estimation, priority Medical Visa Invitation Letters (VIL), airport transfers, local accommodation, and a dedicated 24/7 on-ground care manager in Pune.
